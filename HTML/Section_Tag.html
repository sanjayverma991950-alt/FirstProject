R.R. Institute of Modern Technology (RRIMT) is a private technical institution located at Bakshi Ka Talab on Sitapur Road, Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh. The institute was established in 2008 and is part of the R.R. Group of Institutions. It is approved by AICTE and affiliated with Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow. The institute offers various technical and professional programs, including B.Tech, MBA, and Polytechnic courses. B.Tech branches include Computer Science and Engineering, Information Technology, Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Electronics and Communication Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Biotechnology, and other technology-related disciplines. RRIMT focuses on both theoretical and practical education. The institute provides laboratories, workshops, seminars, industrial visits, expert lectures, and other activities intended to give students practical exposure. It also highlights facilities such as CNC machines and 3D printers. The campus provides facilities such as classrooms, laboratories, a library, sports facilities, a cafeteria, hostel accommodation, transportation, seminar halls, and conference facilities. Overall, R.R. Institute of Modern Technology is an engineering and management institution in Lucknow that provides students with opportunities to develop technical knowledge, practical skills, and professional abilities.
